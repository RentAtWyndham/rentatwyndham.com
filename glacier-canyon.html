Check-in facts

Check in at the Club Wyndham desk

Where to check in

Glacier Canyon is a Club Wyndham resort inside the larger Wilderness Resort. Go to the Club Wyndham check-in desk in the Glacier Canyon Lodge building - not the Wilderness Hotel desk or the Glacier Canyon Lodge hotel desk, which cannot see Club Wyndham reservations. Bring a photo ID that matches the name on the reservation (21 or older). No need to meet us at the resort.

Security deposit

The resort holds a refundable $250 security deposit on your card at check-in, like any hotel, and releases it 1 to 3 days after checkout.

Your confirmation

Wyndham emails the official guest confirmation to you once the reservation is in your name. Bring it or just bring your ID.
