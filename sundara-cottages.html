What is included

Spa access, wristbands and no resort fee

  • All spa and water amenities at no charge, two guests at a time - your group can take turns through the stay
  • Waterpark wristbands included with the reservation
  • No resort fee
  • Free self parking
  • Free Wi-Fi for up to 4 devices
  • Service animals welcome
Good to know

Before you book

Children are welcome in the cottage

The spa is for guests 18 and older. That age limit applies to the spa only - children may stay in the cottage with you.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM.

Where it is

920 Canyon Road, Wisconsin Dells, WI 53965 - the same address as Sundara Inn and Spa.

Looking for the waterpark lodge instead?

Our flagship property is Glacier Canyon Lodge at the Wilderness, also in Wisconsin Dells, with 1 to 4 bedroom condos. When Sundara is booked for your dates we will tell you what is open there.
