How it works

Three steps. Usually done the same day.

We already hold Club Wyndham reservations at Glacier Canyon. You tell us your dates, we match you to a suite, and the reservation goes into your name.

1

Tell us your dates

Use the request form or email info@rentatwyndham.com with your dates, how many adults and kids, and the suite size you want. We answer personally, usually within the hour.

2

Review your invoice

We send a QuickBooks invoice that lists the dates, the suite, the total price, payment terms, and the cancellation policy. Nothing is due until you have read it.

3

Pay and it's yours

Payment secures the reservation. It goes into your name and Wyndham emails you the official guest confirmation. Check in at the front desk with your photo ID like any hotel.

Good to know

Questions we get every week

Can you hold dates for me?

No. Every reservation is first come, first served, and payment is what secures it. If you want a weekend, the invoice is the fastest way to lock it.

Is there a deposit or resort fee?

We do not charge one. Like any hotel, the resort holds a refundable $250 security deposit on your card at check-in and releases it 1 to 3 days after checkout.

Are waterpark passes included?

Yes. Every registered guest on the reservation gets a Wilderness waterpark wristband for the length of the stay.
