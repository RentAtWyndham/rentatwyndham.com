FAQ

Good questions

Does it cost me anything?

No. There are no fees and no costs to you, ever. You're paid; you're never billed.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. You're never locked into anything. We see this as a partnership - if it stops working for you, we talk about it.

What if I want to use my points myself?

Your own travel comes first. Just tell us about any personal reservations, and if you need points back, we can make them available to you quickly.

Does my home resort matter?

Tell us what you own and we'll talk it through on our call. Owners with several contracts are welcome.

Who will I be dealing with?

John Ellis or another one of our owners. A real person, every time - no bots or call centers.

Are you Wyndham?

No. RentAtWyndham is owners helping owners rent their points. We are not Wyndham and are not affiliated with Wyndham or the resorts, and we can't speak for Wyndham's policies.
